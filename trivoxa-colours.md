**Primary**

**Dark website**

* Background: `#241C18`

* Background: `#F4EFE6`  
* Logo/text: `#241C18`

**Deep sections**

* Background: `#171210`

**Bronze**

* `#A88B68`  
* Use **sparingly** for tiny accents, hover states, lines, icons, or premium details.
